MEHER MAHEL FIT DIRECTORY

Abaya Sizing Guide

Abayas are sized by length (shoulder to hem). Use your height to find your perfect drape.

Abaya Size Recommended Height Length (in / cm) Bust (in)
Size 52 5'0" – 5'2" (152–157 cm) 52 in / 132 cm 40 in
Size 54 5'3" – 5'4" (160–163 cm) 54 in / 137 cm 42 in
Size 56 5'5" – 5'6" (165–168 cm) 56 in / 142 cm 44 in
Size 58 5'7" – 5'8" (170–173 cm) 58 in / 147 cm 46 in
Size 60 5'9"+ (175+ cm) 60 in / 152 cm 48 in

💡 Boutique Fit Advice:

If you plan to wear high heels, consider sizing up by one length (e.g. from 54 to 56) to ensure the abaya gracefully brushes the shoe without breaking the silhouette.
